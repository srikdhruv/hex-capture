Pilot notes (live co-play, Swiss R9-R14 + knockout)
====================================================
Knobs: control.json {"engine_args","force","veto","prefer_wait_ms","note"}; engine_args apply from the NEXT engine call.
Engine knobs: alpha (star value/ply), beta, bookMargin, hw, minScale/vulnK/reachD (enemy-star scaling), --nobook.

R8 (lost 0-84 as Red vs Eris, wall turtle): opp kept 6 stars sealed by ~96 walls (3-wall line stamps),
attacked with a 6-plain line; ate 14 of our stars at x6. Our 157 stars were pure liability.
Lesson: once enemy stars are sealed, alpha->0 and keep our stars out of reach of their longest piece.

R9 (lost 52-156 as Blue vs aryan, wall builder #2): opp edited 6x early (stars into #1 = flower + 4-star tail,
#2 = 3-wall line), then stamped walls every action (63 walls by ply 83) around its 10 stars.
- Our only capture: ply 43-44 breach+snipe (3@2,2 3@2,1) on their front flower, 52 pts. All other holes walled off by ~ply 50.
- They scored with WALL stamps (walls erase adjacent plains/stars): 10/stamp early; late they stamped their 6-star #1
  (9 -> 30 stars) and then took 2 stars x20 and 1 x30. Their late multiplier jump is the danger.
- Engine middlegame edits wrecked Fortlite: 48 moved #1's star into #2 (5th, exposed star), 56 deleted the #3 sniper,
  later shuffles. Lab says never do these. Now an autopick daemon replaces engine edits touching #2/#3 after ply 20
  unless a capture is on the board.
- Lessons: vs wall builders the crack must come before ~ply 40 (their front flower). After they seal, every exposed
  star of ours near their walls is -multiplier; build far away only (engine did run NE, good) and refill holes.
Pilot mechanics: prefer_wait_ms=3000 + autopick daemon answering every pick instantly = conditional rules at ~0 clock.
Stale pick-<ply>.json files from an old game can be replayed in the next game: daemon deletes them.

R10 (WON 5613-1403 as Red vs wernerolaf, RUSHER): from ply 0 they stamped their 5-star #2 straight at us and ate our
#1/#3 flowers during our book edits (0-112, stars 2 vs 39 at ply 17). At ply 13 the book edit was 84 worse than the
best move but bookMargin=100 kept it. bookMargin=10 from ply 15 -> engine stamped Fortlite #2 + sniped their ~60 open
stars; led from ~ply 50. Lesson: rushers leave huge open star masses; sealed Fortlite #2 + star sniper wins the
exchange even from 2 stars. Start every game with bookMargin=30 (control.json default now).

HANDOFF (pilot #1 -> next pilot)
- Tools (on disk, not committed; client/pilot/ is gitignored): client/pilot/tools/
  * autopick.py: daemon. For every new decision.json it immediately writes pick-<ply>.json (the engine move, or a rule
    move), so prefer_wait_ms=3000 costs ~0 clock. It deletes stale pick-*.json (an old game's pick-<ply> could otherwise be
    replayed in the next game). Rules in tools/rules.json:
      {"<ply>": {"move": "...", "unless_capture": true}}  conditional pick (skipped if any top move captures)
      {"protect_from": 20}  from ply 20, replace engine EDITS touching #2/#3 with the best non-edit top move unless a
                            capture is on the board (stops the engine wrecking Fortlite / deleting the sniper; R9 issue).
    Run: cd client/pilot/tools && nohup python3 -u autopick.py >> autopick.log 2>&1 &   then set prefer_wait_ms 3000.
    If the daemon is NOT running, keep prefer_wait_ms 0 (otherwise every ply waits the full time).
  * opp.py: opponent profile from newest game log (edits/stamps, walls, hands, open/soft/sealed stars, distances).
  * holes.py: enemy stars with empty/plain neighbours + distance from our cells (is a crack still possible?).
  * cap2.mjs: exhaustive 2-placement capture search via reference rules (pruned near enemy stars; can be slow).
- Engine handles tactics well (found the R9 breach+snipe itself). The pilot's levers that mattered: bookMargin vs rushers,
  alpha 0 vs sealed turtles, and stopping destructive post-book edits.
- engine_args take effect from the NEXT engine call; picks/force must be prepared before the decision appears.
